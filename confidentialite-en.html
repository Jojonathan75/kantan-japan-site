Privacy policy

2026-11-24

In short

Kantan Japan collects as little data as possible. No ads, no tracking, no selling of data.

Without an account, everything stays on your phone. With an account, only your email and your progress are saved online.

Who is responsible?

The app publisher: Jonathan Bardavid, sole trader (SIRET 842 485 427 00012), 14 rue Jean Le Galleu, 94200 Ivry-sur-Seine, France. Contact: bardavidjonathan@hotmail.fr · +33 6 98 87 41 55.

Data on your phone

Your nickname, language, companion, settings, progress (lessons, tests, items learned) and Notebook notes are stored only on your device.

Sounds and lesson content are included in the app: no connection is needed.

Online account (optional)

If you create an account, we store your email address and a copy of your progress, so you can get it back on another device.

This data is hosted by Supabase, on servers located in the European Union.

Your email is only used to send you your login code. We never send you advertising.

Friends and ranking: your nickname, companion and number of leaves (days completed) are visible to your friends and, unless you turn it off in Settings, in the world ranking. Your friends also see your day streak and how many kanji and words you have learned. Your email is never shown.

Purchases

The unlimited pass is paid through the App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details: only the information “pass purchased” is stored.

How long?

Data on your phone stays until you uninstall the app or reset your progress.

Account data stays until you delete your account.

Your rights

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → My account → Delete my account: your email and online backup are erased.

You can also request access to, correction of, or a copy of your data by writing to the contact address. You may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Younger users

The app is suitable for all ages. If you are under 15, ask a parent before creating an account.

Changes

This policy may change; the date of the latest update is shown here: 2026-11-24.
