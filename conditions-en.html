The service

Kantan Japan is an app to learn Japanese from N5 to N1: kana, kanji, vocabulary, grammar, tests and reviews.

Free trial and unlimited pass

The app is free to install. Your first 5 days of lessons are free.

After that, the unlimited pass ($9.99 in the United States, €9.90 in France; the store shows the price for your country) unlocks all lessons. It is paid once, with no subscription, and gives lifetime access to the app’s content for as long as the app is offered.

Without the pass, you keep access to what you have already learned to review and take your tests.

Payment, refunds and restoring purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play, under their rules.

Your account

The account is optional. You log in with your email and a 6-digit code. You are responsible for access to your email address.

You can delete your account at any time from Settings.
